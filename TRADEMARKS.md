# NeoGate Trademark Policy

This policy explains how the NeoGate name, logo, and related marks may be used.

NeoGate Community Edition source code is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only. AGPL does not grant trademark rights. The NeoGate name, logo, and related marks are governed by this trademark policy.

## Permitted Uses

You may use the NeoGate name to:

- Refer to the NeoGate project truthfully.
- Describe an unmodified copy of NeoGate.
- Say that your software, service, documentation, or integration is compatible with NeoGate.
- Keep the default "Powered by NeoGate" link in the product interface.
- State that your work is "based on NeoGate" if you have modified the project and clearly identify your own changes.

## Uses That Require Permission

You need prior written permission to:

- Use the NeoGate name or logo as the primary name, logo, or branding of your own product, service, company, domain, or repository.
- Call your modified distribution "NeoGate", "NeoGate Enterprise", "NeoGate Pro", "NeoGate Cloud", "NeoGate Official", or any confusingly similar name.
- Use the NeoGate logo in a way that suggests official endorsement, partnership, certification, or sponsorship.
- Remove or alter attribution in a way that makes a modified distribution look like an official NeoGate release.

## Modified Distributions

If you distribute, host, or provide a modified version of NeoGate and use the NeoGate name, logo, or related attribution, you must make it clear that your version is modified and is not an official NeoGate release.

Recommended wording:

```text
This product is based on NeoGate and includes modifications that are not part of the official NeoGate project.
```

## Logo Use

You may use the NeoGate logo only to identify the NeoGate project or an unmodified NeoGate distribution. Do not use the NeoGate logo as your own product logo or service mark without permission.

## Other Trademarks

All third-party trademarks are the property of their respective owners.
